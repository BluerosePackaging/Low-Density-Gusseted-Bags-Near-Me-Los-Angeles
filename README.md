## Select the Right Film Gauge

A thicker bag is not automatically the right bag.

Film gauge should reflect the weight and characteristics of the product. Lightweight merchandise may require a different construction than heavy industrial components or products with corners that could place stress on the material.

Handling also matters. A product that will be moved repeatedly through a warehouse may need a different specification from an item placed directly into retail storage.
